About This Translation Tool

This tool is designed specifically for Chinese for Affirmative Action (CAA / 华人权益促进会), a San Francisco-based civil rights organization. It translates English content into Chinese while consistently using CAA's preferred institutional terminology.

Rather than generic machine translation, this assistant is aware of how CAA refers to itself, its programs, partner organizations, and key policy concepts — ensuring your translated materials match the voice and terminology already established on caasf.org.

Glossary-Aware

Knows CAA's preferred translations for org names, programs, and policy terms sourced from caasf.org

Both Variants

Supports Simplified (简体, mainland/immigrant community) and Traditional (繁體, Taiwanese/HK diaspora)

AI-Powered

Contextual AI translation with a custom CAA system prompt — not word-for-word substitution

Usage tips: For best results, translate one paragraph at a time. Paste full sentences including context — the AI uses surrounding text to choose the right register and phrasing. Organization names from the glossary will always be rendered using CAA's preferred translation.
